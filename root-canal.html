Service Overview

Relieve Pain, Save Your Tooth.

A root canal saves a severely damaged or infected tooth rather than extracting it. At Art Dent Clinic, we use modern numbing techniques and rotary technology to ensure the procedure is quick, stress-free, and completely comfortable.

The Treatment

What Does Root Canal Therapy Include?

A thorough procedure designed to remove infection and permanently protect your tooth.

01

Diagnosis & Numbing

Digital X-rays identify infection, followed by precise local anesthesia for zero discomfort.

02

Infection Cleaning

Infected pulp tissue is gently removed and root canals are fully disinfected.

03

Canal Sealing

The clean root system is filled with a durable bio-compatible material to stop bacteria.

04

Crown Protection

A custom porcelain crown is placed to restore full structural strength and appearance.
